## 0. Rules for this group (read first)

**Metric definitions (DsrInput.StatsJson)**
- `ev` = log2 of the geometric-mean linear luminance.
- `clip` = % of pixels with **any** channel >= 250.
- `crush` = % of pixels with luma8 <= 3.
- `mean_sat` = mean HSV saturation over all pixels.
- `mid_oklab_a/b` = mean over pixels with luma8 40..200.
- `spread` = log2((p90+1)/(p10+1)). The schema does not include it; this bible adds it as the "modelling" proxy.

**Noise**
- Outdoors EV noise is about ±0.1, and bonfire flicker can reach 0.18 EV. An intended EV move must therefore land at least 0.15 EV from vanilla.
- Oklab: a change within ±0.004 counts as "hold". An intended cast shift must be at least 0.005.

**Sun levels.** Sun radiance = 2·(colA_s/100)^2.2, capped at 0.6. White `_s` colour, gain 2. These values follow README section 4: the current validator rejects LightBank colA_s 34-45 and 56-60 on rows whose s_LightBank is 100.
| level | colA_s | radiance |
|---|---|---|
| off | 0 | 0 |
| low | 15–25 | 0.03–0.095 |
| mid | 30–33 | 0.14–0.17 |
| high | 50–55 | 0.44–0.54 |

Snow stays excluded from the sun (`data/sun_ab.json`: the sun on snow raises clip by about 1.7 points).

**Readability floor.** The cap ratio grayKey/minAdaptedLum must stay at or above 0.78× vanilla (1 EV / model_gain 2.8). Limits at vanilla min / vanilla grayKey:
| ToneMap row | grayKey must stay >= | minAdaptedLum must stay <= | extra condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Painted, a11 row 0 (0.10/0.06) | 0.078 | 0.077 | |
| Darkroot, a12 row 0 (0.14/0.11, max 0.13) | 0.109 | — | never raise min above max 0.13. The 1.4× band would exceed it, so min is effectively not a lever here. |
| DLC, a12 rows 11 and 20 (0.12/0.02) | 0.094 | 0.0256 | |

**Sensitivity caveats**
- None of the four sweep views is in G2, so every response figure below is extrapolated from firelink/parish/anor_interior/archives_balcony.
- **Darkroot exposure is effectively locked** (minAdaptedLum 0.11 against maxAdapredLum 0.13). Ambient, envDif and scatter edits land almost fully in EV there. Expect the top of each sweep range, and step in ×0.05 increments.
- Painted (0.06..0.50) adapts, so it lands nearer the bottom of each range.
- Before tuning, add `darkroot_garden` and `painted` to the next sweep: ambient ×0.8, scatter ×0.8, brightnessR ×0.96, envDif ×0.8.

**Flags for W1 or the spec owner.** None of these is resolved here.
1. **Lockstep vs. character over-lighting.**
   - The most consistent finding across sources is that DSR over-lights characters (sweep A §5; sweep B §1).
   - Under `[lockstep]`, a declared character row may follow its map row's colA ratio only within ±10%. So, within spec: **char colA_u/d = map ratio × 0.90–1.00**. Any deeper character cut needs a W1 exception.
   - **colA_s is also inside the lockstep check.** `validate.py ratio()` returns inf for 0 → nonzero, so turning the sun on in a declared character row whose vanilla colA_s is 0 is an error. That covers a12 char rows 10, 13, 14, 30–32 and 44. **colA_s must be carved out of lockstep** for per-row sun use (README P3/P4).
   - On *undeclared* rows the same edit passes, but only because `inf > inf` is false in the same-ID s_ check. Treat that as needing an explicit declaration, not as allowed.
2. **a12 colA_s above the cap.**
   - Row 0 (Darkroot map/objects, 300) and row 4 (trees, 70) already exceed the shader cap. Left untouched, they render the **full** sun on unlightmapped shadowed objects.
   - Lowering row 0 also cuts the Basin lake's water specular, which reads the same c6 without the sun gain: 11.2 → about 0.33 at colA_s 60, roughly 34× weaker.
   - Row 4 (398 tree objects, no water) can be lowered safely.
   - Row 0 needs a decision, made by A/B: (a) keep 300 and accept capped sun on row-0 props; (b) set it to 60 or below and capture the lake; (c) have the shader team scale the sun separately from water.
   - **Resolved (W1):** option (c), as shader gate S1. Rows above 60 in vanilla get no sun and keep their specular. Row 0 stays 300. Until S1 exists, no sun build ships.
3. **Darkroot shadow density 40.**
   - ShadowBank a12 row 0 has densityRatio 40. If ShadowColor tracks densityRatio (the shader note says it is conditional), full shadow likely still passes about 60% of the sun.
   - That light would leak under the canopy and into the Basin cave. Confirm with a sun-on/off crop in the Basin cave.
   - Either build with `DSRLE_SUN_SHADOW_RENORM` or keep the Darkroot sun very low. densityRatio is not a listed lever, so do not change it without a band.
4. **Missing bands.** No band exists for LightScatteringBank sunR/G/B/A/blendCoef/distanceMul or for FogBank fogBeginZ/EndZ. Self-imposed limits for this group: **sunA 0.7–1.3×, scatter RGB ±40, blendCoef ±10, fog distances unchanged.**
5. **DLC row mapping is unverified.**
   - Place-name IDs 1210–1216 are bare numbers (`data/locations.json` does not exist).
   - The candidate rows below assume 1210 Sanctuary Garden, 1211 Oolacile Sanctuary, 1212 Royal Wood, 1213 Township, 1214 Chasm, 1215 Battle of Stoicism and 1216 Township Dungeon. **Confirm by MSB lookup or a one-row tint test before tuning.**
   - a12 **row 0 of Light/Fog/Scatter/ToneMap/ToneCorrect** is also used by m12_01 collisions (places 1212 and 1215). Darkroot grading edits therefore bleed into Royal Wood and Battle of Stoicism; spot-check one Royal Wood view.
6. **Boundaries.** At every crossing the exposure jump must stay within the vanilla jump + 0.3 EV:
   - Parish → Darkroot Garden (G1; called harsh in vanilla by mod 711).
   - Darkroot Garden ↔ Basin.
   - Basin ↔ Valley of Drakes (G5).
   - Painted ↔ Anor Londo (G4).

## 1. Painted World of Ariamis (a11): `painted_entrance`, `painted`

**PTDE mood to recover**
- A colder, **blue-green** cast. fromsoftserve calls the PTDE-valued look the "proper" one (DfgvdVZsRT8).
- PTDE's eye adaptation also flared the snow when you looked up, so bright snow is period-correct. Do not grey it.
- One Steam user reports DSR crushing blacks in the basement (randir14). That view is not ours, but it means: add no crush.
- Confidence: **low**, from a single modder comparison.

**What vanilla shows**
- Cream-yellow snow (mid b +0.026 at the entrance, +0.009 at the bonfire), already slightly green (a −0.03/−0.04).
- 18–27% clip, mostly snow, R-led because ToneCorrect contrastR 1.23 > B 1.08 warms the highlights.
- p10 = 2, with 10% crush in the rock shadows.
- The only warm accent is the torch-lit archway at the top of the stairs.
- The bright character, lit by char row 10 at ambient 100/100 against map 40/50, sits on the "detached character" complaint.

**DSR fidelity and modern lighting to add**
- A **low, cool sun** on characters and props, matching the existing real-time shadow direction (ShadowBank 50/−40, density 85).
- Keep DSR's crisp snow and rock detail; do not add clip.
- Optional: a slightly longer shadow range (endDist 30 → 40–50) so tree and railing shadows hold across the courtyard. Run a shimmer check.
- Optional: a soft bloom lift for the painterly glow (bloomMul 9 → 10–12), only while clip stays in budget.

| painted | −0.25..+0.10 | 0.95..1.08 | −0.006..+0.002 | **−0.014..−0.004** | +0.5 | +0.3 | hold ±0.3 (6.41) | low |

**Levers** (rows: Light 0 map, 1 indoor, chars 10/11/12; Fog 0/1; Scatter 0; ToneMap 0; ToneCorrect 0, with boss rows 1/2 mirrored)
- **Colour, main lever: ToneCorrect 0.**
  - brightnessR 0.90 → 0.86–0.88 and brightnessB 0.90 → 0.92–0.95 (band 0.85–1.15×). Luma impact is under 0.05 EV.
  - Optionally contrastR −0.01..−0.03 and contrastB 0..+0.02. Keep |Δ| ≤ 0.03; contrast is the strongest EV/crush lever in the sweep.
  - Copy the same deltas to ToneCorrect 1/2 (Priscilla) so the boss room does not jump.
- **Round 2 only, after the user signs off on the round-1 colour:**
  - Ambient hue, LightBank 0/10/11: colR_u/d −10..−25, colB_u/d up to 255 (abs 40). Mirror s_LightBank.
  - Fog 0: colR −5..−10, colB 0..+10. Strength degRotW stays as is (the sweep shows fog ×1.6 adds clip and cuts saturation).
- **Scatter 0:** already green (160/225/165). At most sunR −10; sunA unchanged.

  - colA_s 50 → 20–25 on rows 0 and 11. Row 10's s_LightBank is 140, so row 10 can only go to ≤ 23.
  - Use a cool hue: colR_s 230–245, colG_s 245–255, colB_s 255.
  - Rows 12 and 13 stay 0.
  - Set char/map colA_s in the same ratio, or apply the colA_s lockstep carve-out.
- **Character fill:** char 10/11 colA_u/d at map ratio × 0.90–1.00, paired with the sun so the character's EV holds within ±0.1.
- **Snow-clip test lever:** ToneMap 0 maxAdapredLum 0.5 → 0.6–0.8. This is a hypothesis (bright scenes may sit on the max clamp), not a sweep result. Keep it only if clip falls by at least 1 point with EV inside its range.
- A blue shift trades R-clip for B-clip, so **do not count on hue alone to cut clip.**

**Must not change**
- Snow stays bright, near-white and textured, not grey.
- The warm orange torch archway stays as the lone warm note.
- The green scattering tint stays.
- No new crush in the rock shadows (p10 already 2).

## 2. Darkroot Garden and Basin (a12, m12_00): `darkroot_garden`, `darkroot_basin`

**PTDE mood to recover.** **Darker, greener, with blue undertones, and less milky haze.**
- Restored Lighting found DSR Darkroot **hazier** than PTDE and the Switch version (more scattering density and sky haze). It cut the local haze but kept the area foggy (1423 logs).
- fromsoftserve's PTDE-valued Darkroot is very green, blue-undertoned and very dark, and its fog turned greener (DfgvdVZsRT8).
- Mod 711 adds contrast and calls the Parish transition harsh.
- Push Square says DSR kept the murk.
- One unverified Steam post mentions a "green filter".
- **Do not let the group-wide "PTDE was hazier" framing invert this: for Darkroot the haze comes down.** Confidence: **medium**.

**What vanilla shows**
- **garden:** the flattest view in G2. mid_frac 0.98, p10 47 / p90 131, spread 1.46. Lit mossy stone, and a blue-teal milky forest through the doorway.
- **basin:** a cave view (lightmapped rock, bonfire glow), noticeably warm-yellow (b +0.039), crush 9%.
- FogBank row 0 is **off** (degRotW 0). All the haze is LightScattering row 0: sun 100/100/130 (blue-violet), sunA 620, blendCoef 78.
- ToneMap 0 locks exposure at 0.14 / (0.11..0.13).

**DSR fidelity and modern lighting to add**
- Clarity instead of milk: DSR's stone and foliage detail should read at mid distance through a thinner, greener haze.
- Low, canopy-filtered sun on characters and props. The shadow range is already 2000; do not touch it.
- More modelling: baked light against less ambient and env fill.

| darkroot_garden | **−0.40..−0.15** | 0.98..1.10 | **−0.009..−0.002** | −0.008..+0.002 | +0.5 | +1.5 | **+0.15..+0.60** (1.46); p90 ≥ 120 | low |

Both views share every row, so one edit set must satisfy both tables.

**Levers** (rows: Light 0 map, 4 trees, 10 char; Scatter 0; ToneMap 0; ToneCorrect 0; Fog 0 off; Shadow 0)

**W1 cap: v1 uses three levers only.** The full list below is the menu for a later round.
1. Scatter 0: sunA ×0.75–0.90 and a greener colour, halved to sunR −5..−10, sunG +5..+15, sunB −5..−10.
2. ToneCorrect 0 brightnessR 1.00 → 0.96–0.98.
3. LightBank 0 envDif_colA ×0.80–0.90.

The ambient cut, grayKey, saturation and ambient hue moves are not in v1. Char row 10's ambient is quantised to 46–49 anyway (s_ 0).

- **Less fill, more modelling.**
  - LightBank 0: colA_u/d ×0.85–0.95 and envDif_colA ×0.80–0.90.
  - Char 10 colA_u/d at map ratio × 0.90–1.00.
  - Mirror s_LightBank.
  - Extrapolated: ambient ×0.9 ≈ −0.1..−0.3 EV, envDif ×0.85 ≈ −0.1..−0.3 EV. Start there and step.
- **Haze down and greener: Scatter 0.**
  - sunA ×0.75–0.90. The sweep shows ×2 = +0.3..+0.7 EV and −0.16 sat, so the cut also restores saturation.
  - Colour: sunR −5..−15, sunG +10..+25, sunB −5..−20. The aim is teal-green, not neutral.
  - blendCoef −0..−8.
  - FogBank 0 stays off (vanilla degRotW 0, so rel bands cannot move it anyway).
- **Grade: ToneCorrect 0.**
  - brightnessR 1.00 → 0.95–0.98, which is the green shift. brightnessB 1.02 holds.
  - saturation 1.10 → 1.12–1.20 (at most 1.265).
  - No contrast change unless spread is still short after the fill cut, then contrastG +0.01..+0.02 only.
- **Exposure, only if needed: ToneMap 0** grayKey 0.14 → 0.12–0.14 (floor 0.109). Leave min/max alone.
- **Ambient hue:** LightBank 0/10 colR_u/d −10..−25 (greener).
- **Sun:**
  - Row 4 (trees) 70 → 15–25. Reachable: s_LightBank 100 → 30–50.
  - Row 10 (char) stays 0 in v1. Earlier drafts had 0 → 10–20, which needs the colA_s carve-out.
  - Row 0 (300) is spec-only under S1 (flag 2, resolved).
  - Hue colR_s 225–240, colG_s 255, colB_s 225–245.
  - Because of flag 3 (density 40), check the **basin** character region: sun build vs. sun-off ΔEV ≤ +0.05.
- **Overshoot risk:** fill, scatter and grayKey all stack at full strength under the locked exposure. Change one per capture round.
- **Edit order:** scatter sunA, then envDif, then ambient, then grayKey.
  - colA_d is what lifts the Basin cave floor (crush 9.15%, p10 7), so cutting ambient first will hit the Basin crush cap (+1.5) before the Garden reaches −0.15 EV.
  - The Basin crush cap is the stop condition for these shared rows.

**Must not change**
- Darkroot stays foggy and twilit, not clear daylight.
- Moss greens and stone detail stay.
- The bonfire's warm pool stays.
- The basin lake's water specular stays (row 0 colA_s, flag 2).
- The bright open Basin and Hydra lake stay brighter than the Garden.
- The Parish → Garden step must not get harsher (flag 6).

## 3. Oolacile: Sanctuary Garden and Oolacile Sanctuary (a12, m12_01): `sanctuary_garden`, `oolacile_sanctuary`

**PTDE mood.** **No source describes how PTDE looked here.** Restored Lighting found literal PTDE values wrong in the Mausoleum, Royal Wood and Township, and went back to DSR's values (1423 logs). Mod 711 only cuts the player's glow.

So this section follows the established art intent: an ancient, sunlit, overgrown realm, with a gold-green primeval root forest (Sanctuary Garden) and a bright, idyllic sun-dappled clearing at the Sanctuary. It is the last light before the Abyss. **Hold near vanilla; fidelity-only moves.** Confidence: **low**.

**What vanilla shows**
- **sanctuary_garden:** a dark root tunnel (EV −5.0). It is the **most saturated view in G2** (0.73), gold-green (b +0.030).

- Characters are flat silver: their char rows have colA_s 0, so no key light.

**DSR fidelity and modern lighting to add.** A sun on characters and props that matches the baked sun patches: mid in the clearing, low under the roots. Nothing else.

## 4. Oolacile Township and Township Dungeon (a12, m12_01): `oolacile_township`, `oolacile_dungeon`

**PTDE mood.** **No PTDE comparison source exists.** Restored Lighting kept DSR's Township values; mod 711 cuts the player's glow in the first tunnels and the open area. By art intent this is a grand town decaying under the Abyss: overcast, wet stone, deep shadow under the arches. The dungeon is near-black, lit by the player and a few sources. Confidence: **low**.

**What vanilla shows**
- **township:** flat (spread 2.06, p10 43). The strong dark Village fog (row 23: degRotW 130, colour 37/36/40) lifts the shadows. Slightly blue (b −0.010).
- **dungeon:** EV −9.45, crush 45%, clip 9%. The near walls glare white-blue around the player while the corridor is black. This matches the much-repeated "DSR player light too strong" finding (sweep A §5; sweep B §1).

**DSR fidelity and modern lighting to add**
- Township: a little more depth from less env-diffuse fill, and a low sun on characters. No shadow-range change: ShadowBank 20 spans places 1211–1216, so it is not Township-local.
- Dungeon: readability only. Calm the near-wall glare if our levers can reach it.
