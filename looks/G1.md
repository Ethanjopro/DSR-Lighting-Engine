> - **Colour.** The colour moves are halved or moderated, because their sources are unverified:

>   - firelink a −0.004..+0.002 and b −0.005..+0.001;
>   - Burg fog colR 43-48, colB 75-80, degRotW 48-54;
>   - Burg ToneCorrect/Scatter cooling is round 2 only.
>
>   The first colour capture is a user sign-off.
> - **Parish.** No Scatter 11 or LightBank 16 edits: both are shared with Firelink (m10_02).
> - **depths_dragon.** EV −0.20..+0.05. The character crop keeps −0.90..−0.15.
> - **Depths and the pool.** LightBank 3 colA_d is 16 or 17 (18 is unreachable). Rows 2/3/4 (100/200/300) stay vanilla as spec-only once the shader gate S1 exists, so this bible's colA_s decision is replaced by S1.
> - **Pairs.** New pair checks: depths_dragon ↔ blighttown_bridge and parish ↔ darkroot_garden.

Target: "PTDE moods, DSR fidelity". G1 has the best PTDE evidence of any group: one modder read Burg FogBank row 10 aloud for both versions. Even so, every value below is a **relationship** to recover (cooler fog, less flat fill, a glow on light sources), never a PTDE number. Both PTDE-oriented mods found that the same parameter renders differently in the two engines. Where no source exists (Parish Sunlight Altar, both Depths views), the target follows the area's established art intent, and the text says so.

**How to read the targets.** Every delta is measured against `captures/baseline_vanilla/vanilla/<slug>.stats.json`, with the same fixed camera and the HUD masked.
- `EV Δ` is new ev minus vanilla ev.
- `sat ×` is new mean_sat / vanilla mean_sat.
- `a Δ` and `b Δ` are changes in mid_oklab_a and mid_oklab_b. +a is redder/magenta, +b is yellower/warmer.
- `clip Δ` and `crush Δ` are maximum increases in percentage points.
- `P floor` is the lowest allowed p90 (p50 for firelink, whose p90 is already 255).

**Noise.** Repeated vanilla captures differ by 0.03 EV at parish and 0.08 EV at firelink. Mid Oklab differs by ≤0.0015 and sat by ≤0.013. Bonfire flicker moves character crops by up to 0.18 EV (`data/sun_ab.json`). No EV range here is narrower than 0.30, and no a/b range is narrower than 0.004. Judge every view on the mean of 2 captures, because all six G1 views have a bonfire in or near frame. If the shaders are frozen and re-baselined first (PLAN), apply these deltas to the re-baseline, not to the Phase-0 captures.

**Rows shared across G1 maps.** Tune these only with every affected view captured.
- **Row 0 of every bank** is used in m10_00, m10_01 and m10_02. LightBank 0 also carries 294 placed characters, which may be an MSB default the collision row overrides: unverified.
- **FogBank 11, Scatter 11 and LightBank 16** are used in Burg/Parish (m10_01) and Firelink (m10_02). LightBank 16 covers 363 objects. A Parish-interior edit on these rows moves Firelink objects as well.
- **ToneMap, ToneCorrect and Shadow rows 1 and 2**, plus **Fog and Scatter row 1**, are used in both the Depths and Burg/Parish.
- Prefer area-specific rows:
  - Burg/Parish: LightBank 10/11/15/17, Fog/Scatter 10, ToneMap/ToneCorrect 10/11.
  - Firelink: rows 20-29.
  - Depths: rows 2-9.

**Lockstep.** s_LightBank rows must follow every `colA*` ratio of the same-ID LightBank row to within ±10%, including colA_s. Rows that block or quantise this:
- **Row 22:** s is 0/0, so leave its ambient alone.
- **Rows 1, 13, 14:** s is 5-10, so only ratios near ×0.8 or ×0.9 survive rounding.
- **Row 3:** s is 10/5. colA_u can take any value in ×0.6-1.4. colA_d (20) can take only 16 or 17, because s goes 5→4: 18 (×0.9) fails.
- **Rows 33 and 36:** s is 5/5 and 5/10.

Route fill cuts on those rows through `envDif_colA`, which is not lockstepped.

s_LightBank colA_s is 100 on rows whose LightBank value is 50. Under the colA_s band (max 60, which validate.py also applies to s_LightBank), the reachable LightBank values are:
- 0-33, with s at twice the value;
- 46-55, with s left at 100.

34-45 and 56-60 fail (README section 4.1).

Declared char-row lockstep (PLAN: char rows track map rows ±10%) allows a small character-only trim. Map ×0.90 with char ×0.82 passes. A larger split needs an explicit exception (see Open decisions).

**Directional lights do nothing.** The 421b shaders ignore `colA_0..2` (sweep and probe), and rotations are immutable because the lightmaps are baked. The PTDE sources describe a stronger key against the fill. Here that can only come from two things:
- **less fill:** ambient `colA_u/d` and `envDif_colA`;
- **more key on characters and props:** the new sun (`colA_s`, colour `colR/G/B_s`). The direction comes from ShadowBank lightDegRot (c73), which already agrees with the baked shadows, so there is nothing to tune there.

Cut `colA_d` (ground bounce) more than `colA_u` to get top-down form.

**Darkness cannot come from ToneMap.** The readability floor allows `grayKey/minAdaptedLum` to fall to no less than ×0.78 of vanilla (1.0 EV / model_gain 2.8 = 0.357 EV by ratio). Any PTDE darkness comes from fill cuts. `validate.py` does not see those, so the EV Δ lower bounds above are the only guard. grayKey may go **up** (to ×1.15) to recover readability after a fill cut.

**Contrast is not free here.** At parish, contrast +0.10 cost −2.9 EV and +21 pt crush. Even the band edge (+0.03) costs about 0.5 EV there. Leave `contrastRGB` at vanilla in G1, and get the modelling from the fill ratio instead.

**Sensitivity used for estimates** (`data/sweep_sensitivity.json`, scaled by log2 of the ratio):

| ambient colA_u/d | +2.05 EV, crush −4.7 | +0.58 EV |

Burg, the altar and the Depths were not swept. Use parish as the proxy: all three are dark (EV −4.3 to −6.0) and likely near the minAdaptedLum clamp, where fill changes hit EV almost 1:1. Add burg and depths to the next sweep.

**Grade levers, approximate.** At a typical midtone, `brightnessB ×1.03` gives b −0.002 to −0.004 and a +0.001. `brightnessR ×0.97` gives a −0.002. Cool with B up and R slightly down together, so the shade does not drift purple.

## Undead Burg (burg)

**Vanilla read.** The camera is in a sunken stone nook by the bonfire ladder, looking through an arch to a sunlit street. The palette is grey-green moss stone with a mild yellow midtone (b +0.014). It is already desaturated (sat 0.27; ToneCorrect 10 saturation is 0.83). Shade is even, and the walls carry little form.

**PTDE mood to recover** (evidence: high for fog direction, medium for the rest).
- **Cooler, thinner fog.** fromsoftserve's side-by-side reading of FogBank 10 has PTDE bluer, less red and lighter in weight than DSR (53/67/67, strength 60). Use this as a direction only: the values are hearsay and do not port.
- **Less yellow.** A commenter on a PTDE-grade LUT mod found vanilla Burg/Parish/Firelink yellow-filtered next to PTDE.
- **More shading.** DSRRL found DSR's Burg/Parish fill too strong against the key. It cut indoor upper/lower ambient so that corners and recesses separate again. Mod 711 (not PTDE-targeted) found Burg shade blue-violet. The cooling must therefore go toward blue/cyan, never purple.
- **Counterweight.** At least one player prefers DSR's clearer Burg, and VG247 found the gothic mood intact. The Burg darkens only slightly: it is a daylight area.

**DSR fidelity and modern lighting add.**
- A warm real-time sun on characters and props in the streets, gated by the shadow maps and matching the baked key's hue and yaw.
- Inside the nook the shadow map should block most of it ("low" as seen).
- Keep DSR's clean textures, stable shadows and specular stone. Gain the shading from a lower fill ratio, not from a grade.

**Levers.**
- **LightBank 10:** `colA_u` ×0.88-0.95 and `colA_d` ×0.75-0.85; s10 in lockstep. Est. −0.25 to −0.45 EV.
- **LightBank 11 (indoor):** `colA_u/d` ×0.75-0.85.
- **Char rows 15/17:** within ±10% of row 10. Sun per the table.
- **FogBank 10** (moderated after the critic pass, because the source is single and unverified):
  - `colR` 53 → 43-48, `colB` 67 → 75-80, `colG` ±5;
  - `degRotW` 60 → 48-54;
  - `colA` 140 held.
- **Round 2 only**, if b has not reached −0.002 after round 1 (fill cut, fog and sun) and the user signs off on the colour:
  - **Scatter 10:** remove the violet tint (235/230/255). Set `sunR` to 220-230 and `sunG` to 238-250, keep `sunB`. Hold `sunA` at ×0.9-1.0.
  - **ToneCorrect 10:** `brightnessR` 1.06 → 1.03-1.05 and `brightnessB` 1.06 → 1.07-1.09, with G held. `saturation` ×1.00-1.08 (0.83 → 0.83-0.90): the cooling costs a little chroma, and this pays it back.
- **ToneMap 10:** `grayKeyValue` 0.25 → 0.26-0.28. Expect to need this: the row-10 fill cut alone already reaches the EV floor (−0.40), before the row-11 cut.

**Must not change.**
- Warm sunlit stone against cool shade.
- The daylight readability of the streets.
- The ToneMap 10 light-shaft anchor (`lightShaftPower` 0.03) and the god-ray piece (row 33).
- The warm torches and lanterns.
- Hellkite bridge ShadowBank 13 (`endDist` 70).
- The Capra arena (row 36). Its fill may only go down about 10% (DSRRL), and that view is not checked here.

## Firelink Shrine (firelink)

**Vanilla read.** Bright cyan sky (16% clipped), green grass and moss, and grey-green haze across the ruins. The midtones are already green (a −0.023) and slightly yellow (b +0.013). Characters are evenly lit. ShadowBank 20 `endDist` is only 10, so dynamic shadows end close to the camera.

**PTDE mood to recover** (evidence: medium).
- **Green-blue cast with less red.** fromsoftserve rebuilt Firelink from PTDE values and describes it as green-blue dominated with much lower red, more bloom and heavy DoF, with strong contrast kept. The LUT commenter found vanilla Firelink yellow-filtered.
- **Less flat character light.** DSRRL found DSR lights characters at Firelink flatly and uniformly, and cut ambient in some profiles.
- **No stronger light shafts.** DSRRL first boosted god rays here and later reverted to DSR's scattering.
- **Longer shadows.** Mod 711 found the vanilla shadow distance too short.
- **Direction: hold or slightly deepen, not a swing.** The vanilla cyan scatter (70/200/220) and the green midtone already sit on the PTDE side.

**DSR fidelity and modern lighting add.**
- Shadows reach twice as far.
- A neutral-to-cool real-time sun on characters.
- A modest bloom lift as the PTDE "glow". DoF is not ours: DofBank is immutable.

**Levers.**
- **Sun: mid** (rows 20/25/23 colA_s 30-33 with s_LightBank 60, row 27 ≤ 20). The sun fades out with the shadow range, so it is paired with the endDist change below.
- **ShadowBank 20:** `endDist` 10 → 18-20. Scale `fadeBeginDist` (3) and `fadeDist` (7) by the same ratio. Check for shimmer, since perspective shadow maps trade resolution for range. Est. −0.06 to −0.12 EV.
- **LightBank 20:** `colA_u/d` ×0.88-0.95.
- **Char 25/27:** ×0.80-0.88, paired so the char ratio stays between 0.9× and 1.1× the map ratio (map ×0.88 → char ≥ ×0.79; map ×0.95 → char ≥ ×0.86), or lockstep fails. Est. −0.05 to −0.11 EV on the frame, and more on the character crop, which the sun refills.
- **ToneMap 20:**
  - `bloomMul` 30 → 36-42 (×1.2-1.4), `bloomMulFar` 100 held up to ×1.1. Est. +0.03 to +0.06 EV, clip +0.15 to +0.3.

  - `lightShaftPower` stays 0.
- **ToneCorrect 20:** `saturation` ×0.95-1.02 (it is already 1.2), `brightnessR` 1.0 → 0.98-0.99, `brightnessB` 1.0 → 1.01-1.02. This is halved, because the green-blue claim rests on a single unverified source.
- **Fog 20:** density held (`degRotW` 60-70). Colour 95/95/92 → `colR` 88-93, `colG` 95-100, `colB` 95-100.
- **Scatter 20/22/23:** unchanged.

**Must not change.**
- The cyan sky and the scattering/god rays (DSRRL kept vanilla).
- The bonfire as the warm focal point.
- The hub's readability.
- Frampt's hole (Fog 25) and the dark Fire Keeper cave (row 35).
- The Abyss-entrance look (rows 24/29).

### depths_dragon: Gaping Dragon's room (reservoir)

**Vanilla read.** A cistern with teal murk (a −0.040, sat 0.70) and a golden patch of skylight on the floor. ToneCorrect 4 is deliberately teal (brightness G 1.05 / B 1.10, contrast R 1.34). The knight glows blue-white, lit by character row 9's upper ambient of 120, three times the map row's 40.

**PTDE mood to recover.**
- No source describes this room. The target is the art intent: the teal murk stays, and the character sits in it instead of glowing.
- The character cut follows the general cross-source finding (DSRRL, mod 711, Steam) that DSR characters are over-lit, and DSRRL's note that Depths characters are already bright enough.

**DSR fidelity and modern lighting add.**
- No sun. The skylight patch is baked, and an interior sun would leak.
- Optional: a slight emphasis on the skylight shaft (`lightShaftPower` 0.01 → 0.01-0.03, within the vanilla anchor range).

**Levers.**
- **Char 9:** `colA_u` 120 → ×0.70-0.85. Start at ×0.85 and step down until the character crop reaches its target. c7 scales as colA^2.2, so ×0.70 is ×0.46 on the upper ambient. This needs the lockstep exception below, or a matching cut of map row 4 (which would darken the room instead).
- **ToneMap 4:** `lightShaftPower` as above.
- **Fog 4:** `degRotW` is 0, so the fog lever is dead here.
- **ToneCorrect 4:** held.
- **colA_s on row 4** (300, water): spec-only under S1, as for row 3. The pool specular is the asset at risk.
- **Frame EV** −0.20..+0.05. The cut is character-only, so the frame should barely move. The character crop carries the change (−0.90..−0.15).

**Must not change.**
- The teal colour of the room (a stays negative).
- The golden skylight patch.
- The dark water.
- The Gaping Dragon fight's readability.

## Open decisions (for the global model and the shader side)

1. **Character-only fill trims.** The strongest cross-source finding is that characters are over-lit relative to the environment. G1 needs one exception beyond ±10% lockstep: Depths char row 9, `colA_u` ×0.70-0.85 with map row 4 unchanged. Firelink fits inside ±10%. Resolved as proposal P4 in the README: an asymmetric char tolerance of [0.65, 1.10] × the map ratio.
2. **colA_s as both water specular and sun.** Depths rows 2/3/4 cannot keep their specular and lose the sun while both read c6. Resolved as shader request S1 in the README: no sun where the carrier is above the cap (vanilla colA_s above ~60).
3. **LightBank row 0 characters.** Probe whether the 294 placed characters on row 0 actually render with it. If they do, row 0's colA_s needs a Depths-safe value (0-30).
4. **Sweep coverage.** Add burg and depths to the next sensitivity sweep. All Burg and Depths estimates above are parish-proxy numbers.
