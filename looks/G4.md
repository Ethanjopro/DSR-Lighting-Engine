# G4 look bible: Sen's Fortress, Anor Londo, dark Anor Londo (a15)

> **W1 corrections (2026-10-30).** `looks/targets.json` is the checked copy of every number below, and `looks/README.md` holds the shared model. Changes after the critic pass:
> - **Sun reachability.** On rows whose s_LightBank colA_s is 100, the validator accepts LightBank colA_s 0-33 (s_ at 2×, ≤ 60) or 46-55 (s_ left at 100). 34-45 and 56-60 fail.
>   - **anor_entrance (high):** 50-55 with s_ untouched. The old "45-58, s_ 90-116" is not buildable. Fallback: tint-only at 50, if the character rim light visibly disagrees with the sun disc. Shadow and scatter directions differ by 35.5° there.
>   - **sens_bridge (mid):** 30-33 on rows 0/10 (s_ 60).
> - **anor_entrance grading.**
>   - The clip goal is quantified: clip must **fall** by at least 1 point (aim 1-4).
>   - The lever is ToneCorrect 10 brightness ×0.88-0.93. maxAdapredLum is a near-null lever (sweep ×0.5: 0..+0.10 EV).
>   - No bloomMul or lightShaftPower increase.
>   - Hue is held: \|Δa\|, \|Δb\| ≤ 0.006. The warmth claim is contested and comes from an unfinished build. The Scatter/Fog/ambient warming moves to a signed-off round 2.
> - **sens.** EV −0.40..0.00, crush +2.0, p50 ≥ 41, p10 may drop by at most 2. minAdaptedLum is a last resort only (ToneMap 0 is shared with Anor Londo). New evidence: a ResetEra poster recalls the original Sen's with starker contrast and baked lighting, which supports the darker, more shadowed target.
> - **Pairs.** New: sens_bridge ↔ anor_entrance within ±0.30.
> - **No-op edits** are a validator *warning*, not a rejection.
> - **Dark Anor Londo.** colA_s 0 on slot-1 rows reproduces vanilla, so that edit can ship before the dark baseline exists.

Target: "PTDE moods, DSR fidelity". G4 has little PTDE evidence. Sen's has one modder's notes, lit Anor Londo has one contested claim, and dark Anor Londo has nothing. Where there is no source, the target follows the area's established art intent, and the text says so. Every value here is a relationship to recover. None is copied from PTDE: both PTDE-oriented mods found that the same parameter renders differently in the two engines, so values cannot be ported.

**How to read the targets.** Every delta is measured against `captures/baseline_vanilla/vanilla/<slug>.stats.json`, using the same fixed camera with the HUD masked.
- `EV Δ` is new ev minus vanilla ev.
- `sat ×` is new mean_sat / vanilla mean_sat.
- `a Δ` and `b Δ` are changes in mid_oklab_a and mid_oklab_b. +a is redder/magenta, +b is yellower/warmer.
- `clip Δ` and `crush Δ` are maximum increases in percentage points.
- `P floor` is the lowest allowed p90 (p50 for anor_entrance, whose p90 is already 255).

Noise is about ±0.1 EV outdoors and up to 0.18 EV next to a flickering bonfire (`data/sun_ab.json`). No EV range here is narrower than 0.25. Judge near-bonfire views on the mean of 2 captures.

## a15 traps (read before touching anything)

**Which rows each view uses.** These are probable, inferred from `data/live_rows.json` and the row names. Confirm them with the cbuffer probe at each bonfire before tuning: c7/c8 identify the ambient row, c6 the `_s` row.

| view | ToneMap | ToneCorrect | char Light | map Light | Fog | Scatter | Shadow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sens | 0 | 0 or 1 | 10 / 11 / 12 | 0, 1 | 0, 1 | 0 | 0 or 1 |
| sens_bridge | 0 | 0 | 10 | 0, 2, 3, 4 (trees) | 0, 2, 4 | 0 | 0 |
| anor_entrance | 10 | 10 | 30 | 20, 27, 24/25 (trees) | 10, 14 | 10 | 10 |
| anor_interior | 11 | 11 | 31 (33 in west-sun rooms) | 21, 23, 28 | 10 | 10 | 11 |
| anor_gwynevere | 11 (12 = the chamber beyond the door) | 11 | 31 | 21, 28 | 10 | 10 (11/14 inside the chamber) | 11 |

**Rows shared by both maps** (m15_00 and m15_01 in live_rows):
- ToneMap 0, ToneCorrect 0, Fog 0, Scatter 0, LightBank 0, Shadow 0.
- Fog 10 and Scatter 10. Some m15_00 pieces use them too; live_rows pools the count across both maps.
- So every Sen's lever on a row-0 entry also moves part of Anor Londo, and every Anor Londo lever on Fog/Scatter 10 also moves part of Sen's. Prefer area-specific rows:
  - Sen's: LightBank 1/2/3/4/10/11/12, Fog 1/2, ToneCorrect 1, Shadow 1.
  - Anor Londo: LightBank 20+, ToneMap 10/11, ToneCorrect 10/11, Shadow 10/11.

**Slot-1 mirroring.** Vanilla slot 1 (dark Anor Londo) equals slot 0 for ToneCorrect 0/1, Fog 2, Scatter 1 and s_LightBank 12/23, and `validate.py` keeps them identical. Any Sen's grade edit on ToneCorrect 0/1 therefore lands in dark Anor Londo as well.

**Lockstep quantisation.** s_LightBank rows must follow every `colA*` ratio of the same-ID LightBank row to within ±10%, including colA_s. Some s_LightBank fields are zero or tiny and cannot follow a ratio, so changing `colA_u/d` on the matching LightBank row fails validation:
- Slot 0: row 23 (0/0), row 30 (colA_u 0), rows 34 and 35 (1/1).
- Slot 1: row 34 (0/0).
- Row 21 (3/3) can only take ratios that 3 rounds to, e.g. ×0.667 (3→2).

Route fill cuts on those rows through `envDif_colA`/`envSpc_colA` instead, which are not lockstepped.

s_LightBank colA_s is 100 on every row:
- colA_s 50→0 on LightBank means 100→0 on s_LightBank.
- 50→55 leaves s_LightBank at 100, which is within ±10%. Moving s_ to 110 would break the max-60 band, which validate.py also applies to s_LightBank.
- 34-45 and 56-60 are unreachable (README section 4.1).

**Directional lights do nothing.** The 421b shaders ignore `colA_0..2` (sweep and probe). The "key vs fill" balance that the PTDE sources describe can only come from two things:
- **less fill:** ambient `colA_u/d` and `envDif_colA`;
- **the new sun:** `colA_s` on draws without a lightmap, i.e. characters, objects and props. Lightmapped walls and floors are gated off from it.

**Readability floor.** It is checked on the ToneMap rows only, with model_gain 2.8. The combined change in grayKey/minAdaptedLum must stay ≥ ×0.78 of vanilla: minAdaptedLum at most ×1.28, or grayKey at least ×0.78. ToneMap 0 is shared with Anor Londo. Darken with lighting cuts first and with exposure only as a last resort.

**Sun tint cap.** colR/G/B_s have an abs-40 band, so the warmest sun is about 255/215/215. LightScatteringBank sun RGB has no band in global.toml. This bible caps it instead, per view below.

## Sen's Fortress (m15_00, rows 0-12): confidence low

**PTDE mood to recover.**
- fromsoftserve rebuilt Sen's from PTDE values. He reports the interior becomes very dark and depends on the player's lantern, as PTDE did.
- He also says DSR's Sen's and Burg palettes are already similar to each other.
- Mod 711, which is not PTDE-targeted, toned down character glow at the bridge entrance and retuned contrast, fog and sky brightness.
- So the mood to recover is a darker, more shadowed fortress interior with less fill on characters. It is not a colour shift.
- No source describes Sen's fog or hue. By analogy with the Burg (fromsoftserve read a10 fog row 10: PTDE bluer and weaker), at most a slight cool drift is permitted. It is not required.

**DSR fidelity / modern lighting to add.** Keep DSR's sharper shadows and stone specular. Let the overcast sun model characters on the open walkways at low or mid strength. Extend outdoor shadow range so the sun is shadowed further out.

**Must not change.**
- Readability of traps: pendulum blades, spear and arrow traps, the boulder ramps. Hence the crush and p90 limits.
- The grey-green mossy stone and the green forest at the bridge.
- ToneCorrect 0's per-channel contrast (R 1.15 > B 1.02). This is the vanilla cool-shadow/warm-highlight split.
- ToneMap 0 lightShaftPower 0.07, the only non-zero shaft value in a15.

### sens (bonfire enclosure)

Ambiguous view: stone walls with ivy, lit from the sky above, spears in the floor. Sun = **low**, but the real knob is which character row the collision selects. Row 10 (outdoor) gets colA_s 15-25. Rows 11 (indoor) and 12 (shade) are always 0.

- **Fill, the main lever.**
  - Char rows 10/11/12: colA_u/d ×0.75-0.85.
  - Row 10: envDif_colA 130 → 105-115 and envSpc_colA 130 → 105-120. This cuts the sourceless sheen.
  - Map row 0: colA_u/d ×0.8-0.9 and envDif_colA 120 → 100-110. This is a shared row, so run the cross-check.
  - Map row 1 is already at 5/10. Leave it.
  - s_LightBank 10/11/12 get the same ratios. Row 12 is slot-mirrored in s_LightBank.
- **Exposure, only if EV is still above the range.** ToneMap 0 minAdaptedLum 0.045 → at most 0.055 (×1.22). The model cost of that is 0.81 EV, under the 1.0 floor.
- **Grade.** ToneCorrect 0/1 saturation 1.0/1.1 → ×1.00-1.06. This is mirrored to slot 1.
- **Fog.** Fog 0 and Fog 1 degRotW ×0.9-1.1. Colour may lose 0-10 R units for a slight cool drift.
- **Bloom.** ToneMap 0 bloomMul ×1.0-1.25 for a softer, PTDE-like glow around light sources. Bloom is near noise in EV.
- **Shadow.** Shadow 1 (indoor) unchanged.
- **Reachability.** This is extrapolated: no sweep was run at a dark Sen's view. Exposure there is probably at the minAdaptedLum cap, so fill cuts should pass through about 1:1. Re-sweep ambient ×0.75 and envDif ×0.75 here first.

### sens_bridge (entrance bridge from the Parish)

Outdoor and overcast, between dense forest. Sun = **mid**: colA_s 30-33 on rows 0 and 10 (s_LightBank 60). Trees row 4 gets at most 15, because foliage beyond the shadow range would be lit without shadows and look flat. Rows 2 and 3 (cliff and middle ground) get 15-25.

- **Character glow (mod 711).** Row 10: colA_u/d ×0.8, and envDif_colA and envSpc_colA 130 → 100-115. The sun adds the modelling back. Check the white armour in the character crop.
- **Forest.** Row 4 ambient hue (195/215/255) and colA 60/30 stay as they are. They carry the green-blue forest. Do not push saturation (vanilla 0.61 is already the highest of the Sen's views).
- **Shadow.** Shadow 0 endDist 40 → 55-70 (≤ 2×). Check for shimmer on the fortress wall. Shadow 0 is shared with Anor Londo, so run the cross-check (shadow range was near noise in the sweep).
- **Fog.** Unchanged, and no denser haze. In the sources, PTDE's "haze" is bloom and DoF glow, not fog density.
- **Shared row.** ToneMap 0 is shared with the sens view, so tune the two views together.
- **Reachability.** Extrapolated, nothing was measured outdoors in Sen's. Ambient ×2 gave +0.58 EV at Firelink (outdoor), so a 20% fill cut should cost roughly 0.1-0.2 EV.

## Anor Londo, lit (m15_01 slot 0, rows 20-35, Fog/Scatter/ToneMap/ToneCorrect 10-12): confidence low

**PTDE mood to recover.**
- fromsoftserve says PTDE values made his Anor Londo much more red and orange. His build was unfinished, and comparison-video comments disagree about whether DSR is warmer or cooler.
- Restored Lighting reduced DSR's character lighting in Anor Londo, more in the interior and basement profiles, but kept DSR's specular on purpose.
- Mod 711 evened out the lantern and retuned the tonemap.
- The Anor Londo Lighting Fix (GitHub) targets washed-out brightness, but it is aimed at both versions.
- So: a slightly warmer, less washed-out sunset outdoors, and less character fill indoors. Keep the warm-stone interiors; there is no PTDE evidence about their colour, so that is art intent.

**DSR fidelity / modern lighting to add.** A strong, warm, low sun on characters and props outdoors, which suits the sunset. Pull back DSR's blown sky so the gradient reads; this is the fidelity win at the entrance. Remove the sun's interior leak (+0.25 EV on characters in `data/sun_ab.json`).

**Must not change.**
- The golden sunset sky and the cathedral silhouette against it.
- The warm cream marble and the Silver Knights' specular. Do not lower envSpc_colA on map rows 20, 21, 23 or 28.
- Gwynevere's doorway glow and the rows behind it: LightBank 34/35, ToneMap 12, ToneCorrect 12, Scatter 11/14.
- ShadowBank 10 endDist 230.
- ToneCorrect hue, which stays 0 everywhere. Hue is a global rotation; do not use it as a grading tool.

### anor_entrance (rooftop after the bats, sun in frame)

Sun = **high**.
- colA_s 50-55 on char row 30 and object rows 27 and 20, with s_LightBank left at 100. Row 20 is lightmapped, so only its unlightmapped draws see the sun.
- Warm the tint inside the band: colG_s 220-235, colB_s 210-225.
- **Direction check.** The shadow-light direction (used by the sun) and the scatter sun disc differ by 35.5° here. If the character rim light visibly disagrees with the disc, fall back to tint-only at 50.

**Pull back the blown sky.** This is the first priority: clip must fall by at least 1 point (aim 1-4).
- ToneCorrect 10 brightness 1.2 → 1.06-1.12 (×0.88-0.93). Est. −0.15..−0.25 EV.
- maxAdapredLum is not the lever: the sweep measured ×0.5 as 0..+0.10 EV.
- Hold grayKey 0.1. Nothing may push EV up here.

**Warmth: round 2 only**, after the user signs off on round 1. Hue is held at |Δa|, |Δb| ≤ 0.006 in round 1. The caps below still apply.
- Scatter 10: sunR stays 255. sunG 150 → 135-150 and sunB 80 → 60-80, i.e. ΔG ≥ -15 and ΔB ≥ -20. sunA ×0.85-1.0, because scatter ×2 cost +2.5 clip points at Firelink.
- Fog 10 colour 135/130/125 → R 140-150, G 125-132, B 105-122. degRotW stays 50 ±10%. Fog 10 is shared with Sen's, so run the cross-check.
- LightBank 20 ambient hue 235/220/220 → R 240-250, B 195-215.
- ToneCorrect 10 saturation 1.2 → 1.20-1.30.

**Glow.** None in v1. bloomMul and lightShaftPower both raise clip, and clip has to fall here. bloomMul stays 30 and lightShaftPower stays 0.

**Characters.** Row 30 envDif_colA 100 → 85-95, so the sun, not the fill, models them. Leave row 30 colA_u alone (s_LightBank 30 colA_u is 0).

**Reachability.** Extrapolated. No bright view was swept. Re-sweep maxAdapredLum ×1.25 and brightness ×0.92 here before tuning.

### anor_interior (bonfire hall, warm marble, bonfire in front)

Sun = **off**. Set colA_s 50 → 0 on every row in the view whose colA_s is 50: 21, 23, 28, 29, 31, 32 and 33. Set s_LightBank to 0 as well. Rows 7, 22, 24 and 25 are already 0; editing them would be a no-op, which validate.py flags as a warning. Row 23 is lightmapped and gated anyway. Row 28's statues probably have no lightmap, so its colA_s matters. Since the vanilla baseline was shot without the sun shader, "off" is zero change against the baseline.

- **Character over-light (Restored Lighting).**
  - Row 31: colA_u/d 25/15 → ×0.70-0.85, with s_LightBank 31 (35/30) at the same ratio.
  - Row 31: envSpc_colA 180 → 120-150. Do not go below ×0.6: Restored Lighting found that vanilla EnvSpec has a per-draw gain that can turn metal black at lighting boundaries.
  - Row 33: colA_u/d ×0.75-0.85, with s_LightBank 33 (15/7) at the same ratio.
- **Flat fill.**
  - Row 23 envDif_colA 280 → 200-250. Use envDif here: row 23's colA cannot be lockstepped.
  - Row 21 envDif_colA 40 stays. If row 21's ambient must drop, use exactly ×0.667 so s_LightBank goes 3→2.
- **Grade.**
  - ToneCorrect 11 saturation 1.1 → 1.00-1.10. This offsets the saturation rise that comes from cutting fill (the sweep measured ambient ×2 as sat -0.20 here).
  - Hold grayKey 0.18. If the view lands below -0.45 EV, grayKey can go up to ×1.10.
- **Reachability.** Measured here: ambient ×2 gave +0.92 EV and envDif ×2 gave +0.60 EV. The cuts above should give about -0.2 to -0.4 EV, which is inside the range.

### anor_gwynevere (stair hall below the Princess's chamber)

Sun = **off**, as for the interior.

**The bright doorway is the focal point and must stay bright.** Don't touch ToneMap 12, ToneCorrect 12 or LightBank 34/35. Clip Δ ≤ +0.5.

The one allowed edit on rows 34/35 is colA_s 50 → 0, with s_LightBank 100 → 0. That keeps the sun out of the chamber and leaves the vanilla look unchanged, because the baseline has no sun.

- **Shared row.** This view shares char row 31 with the interior, so its cut applies here too.
- **Map rows 21 and 28.**
  - envDif_colA ×0.8-0.9.
  - Row 21's ambient changes only at ×0.667.
  - Row 28 is the statue row: colA_u/d 10/6, warm 255/230/200. Hold its hue.
- **ToneMap 11.** bloomMul 70 stays; it is already the soft PTDE-like glow. Fog unchanged.
- **Reachability.** Partly measured: this is the same ToneMap row 11 as anor_interior. Expect about -0.15 to -0.3 EV.

## Dark Anor Londo (slot 1): no source, art intent only

**Mood.** Neither research sweep found any PTDE-vs-DSR source for it. The art intent is the dispelled illusion: a cold, empty, blue-teal night.
- Fog 10 is dense and dark teal: 30/50/55 at degRotW 100.
- Scatter 10 is a weak cyan "moon": 155/245/255, sunA 30.
- ToneCorrect 10-12 brightness is 0.9.
- It must stay readable, because Darkwraiths and the Silver Knights still patrol.

**Shippable now.** colA_s 0 on every slot-1 row reproduces the vanilla look exactly, because vanilla has no sun shader. That one edit does not wait for the baseline.

**Prerequisite for everything else.** There is **no baseline yet**. Capture slot-1 vanilla at the same three Anor Londo bonfires first, which needs a save past Gwynevere (log it in `saves/ledger.json`), as `<slug>_dark`. Add those slugs to `data/bonfires.json` before any check runs against them.

**Provisional targets against that future baseline.**

| view | EV Δ | sat × | a Δ | b Δ | clip Δ | crush Δ | sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| entrance / interior / gwynevere (dark) | -0.25..+0.10 | 0.95..1.05 | -0.004..+0.004 | -0.006..+0.002 (hold cold) | +0.5 | +2.0 | off |

**Levers.**
- Set colA_s to 0 on every slot-1 row that has 50. There is no sun at night.
- Optional later: a cold moonlight on char row 30 only, with colA_s ≤ 15 and tint 215-225 / 235-245 / 255. Do it only after the baseline exists.
- Character rows 30/31/33 slot 1: the same fill-cut logic as lit Anor Londo, with ratios ×0.8-0.9.
- Do not raise Fog 10 degRotW or colA. It is already 100/250.
- Do not lower ToneMap 10/11 grayKey. Slot-1 minAdaptedLum (0.07/0.06, and 0.1 on rows 0/12) is already about twice lit Anor Londo's 0.03/0.04, so the night is already held dark.

**Must not change.**
- The blue-teal fog colour.
- The near-black sky dome (Fog 14: 19/26/25).
- ToneCorrect 0/1, Fog 2 and Scatter 1, which mirror slot 0. Edit them only through Sen's, together with its cross-check.
