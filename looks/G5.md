# G5 look bible: New Londo, Duke's Archives, Crystal Cave, Kiln, Undead Asylum

Archives: `a16` (m16 New Londo), `a17` (m17 Duke's Archives + Crystal Cave), `a18` (m18_00 Kiln, m18_01 Asylum).
Status: W1 draft. Targets are relative to `captures/baseline_vanilla/vanilla/<slug>.stats.json`.

> **W1 corrections (2026-10-30).** `looks/targets.json` is the checked copy of every number below, and `looks/README.md` holds the shared model. Changes after the critic pass:
> - **Baseline.** Guardrails use the re-baseline (frozen shaders + vanilla DrawParam) once it exists. Phase 0 is only for the user-facing before/after.
> - **Sun classes** follow README section 4: low 15-25, mid 30-33, high 50-55. Trap 6 is refined: LightBank 46-55 also passes with s_LightBank left at 100, so only 34-45 and 56-60 are dead.
> - **New Londo row 0** (200, 108 characters, flood water) is spec-only under shader gate S1. That resolves trap 5 and open issue 1. Until S1 exists, no sun build ships.
> - **Archives.** The de-glow goes through **char row 11** (s_ 10, so colA_u ×0.75-0.9 is feasible). Rows 18 and 24 have s_LightBank colA_u 0, which quantises them: row 18 reaches only 55-59 and row 24 only 137-149 (colA_d 91-99). Use envDif_colA ×0.8-0.9 there. The blocker is the zero s_ field, not char-row declaration (README P3).

> - **Dark-view p50 floors:** new_londo_ingward ≥ 28, kiln_gwyn ≥ 24. asylum_2 is checked on its median (crush 36.5%).
> - **Scatter band** (trap 7) is proposed globally as README P1: sunA ×0.7-1.3, RGB abs 40, blendCoef abs 10. This replaces the 0.7-1.4 / ±15 limits used here.

## 1. How to read the targets

- **Deltas.** `dEV` = new ev − vanilla ev. `sat` = new mean_sat / vanilla mean_sat. `da`/`db` = change of mid_oklab_a/b (+a magenta, −a green; +b yellow/warm, −b blue). `clip`/`crush` = maximum allowed rise in percentage points (0 = must not rise; negative values are never required).
- **Floors on every view.** p90 must stay at or above vanilla p90 − 10 (8-bit levels). No ev_delta lower bound in G5 goes below −0.3, well inside the plan's −0.8 limit. This is deliberate: the readability floor (below) allows only ~1 EV of modelled cap drop, and three G5 views are already crushed.
- **Character-crop check (extra, G5-specific).** Use the crop from `data/sun_ab.json`: x 1650, y 880, 560×1180 at 3840×2160. `char dEV` is the change in mean linear luminance in that crop. The most consistent cross-source finding is that DSR over-lights characters, and this crop is the only way a capture can measure it. Bonfire flicker moves this crop by up to 0.18 EV between vanilla runs, so judge it on the median of 3 captures.
- **Noise.** Outdoor EV noise is ~±0.1 and flicker is up to 0.18 EV. Every range below is at least 0.25 EV wide and contains 0, unless the view is meant to move.

## 2. G5-wide findings and rules

**What the evidence supports for G5** (details per area):
- Direct G5 PTDE-vs-DSR evidence is thin.
- The actionable relationship is **characters over-lit indoors** in the Archives (DSRRL). It agrees with the group-wide finding on character and self light (DSRRL, mod 711, Crocalu).
- New Londo has a **do-not-convert** warning. DSR's own compensation is what keeps it visible (DSRRL), and one poster prefers DSR's New Londo.
- Asylum evidence is split. PTDE was flatter and shadowless under open sky (sekai), but others miss PTDE's bloom and depth (Y2Kev, Destructoid).
- Kiln and Crystal Cave: **no PTDE evidence**. Use art intent.
- Lessons from the general notes:
  - PTDE's "haze" is mostly bloom/DoF glow, not denser fog.
  - Parameters do not transfer one-to-one. Literal PTDE values rendered too dark in the Asylum and the later library.
  - Copy relationships (direct vs fill, character vs scene), not numbers.

**Sun (colA_s) mapping.** c6 = (col_s/255 × colA_s/100)^2.2, radiance = 2·c6, capped at 0.6.

| enum | colA_s (buildable today; README section 4) | radiance (white) |
|---|---|---|
| off | 0 | 0 |
| low | 15–25 | 0.03–0.095 |
| mid | 30–33 | 0.14–0.17 |
| high | 50–55 | 0.44–0.54 |

**Every row needs a deliberate colA_s.** Vanilla G5 rows mostly sit at 50, which means sun radiance 0.435 the moment the sun shader ships. Rule: set colA_s = 0 on every G5 LightBank row except the outdoor rows named below.

**Traps the tuner will hit:**
1. **Readability floor.** Model gain 2.8 with max 1.0 EV means grayKey/minAdaptedLum may fall to no less than 0.78× vanilla (e.g. minLum ×1.28 alone, or grayKey ×0.78 alone). The sweep says that costs −0.2 to −1.0 EV on screen. Do not darken by ToneMap in the crushed views (`new_londo_ingward`, `kiln_gwyn`, `asylum_2`).
2. **Ambient is a power-2.2 lever.** c7/c8 = (col × colA)^2.2, so colA ×0.85 cuts the ambient term to ×0.70 and ×0.6 cuts it to ×0.33. Use small ratios on colA_u/colA_d, ×0.75–0.95.
3. **Zero fields under `rel` bands are frozen** (0 × ratio = 0). Affected fields and their substitutes:
   - a18 LightBank 0 (shared Kiln/Asylum base row) and 1 (Kiln towers) colA_u/colA_d = 0. Use envDif_colA or ToneMap instead.
   - a17 FogBank 3 degRotW = 0 (Crystal Cave). There is no fog lever there.
   - a18 ToneMap 23/25/26/27 bloomMul = 0 (Asylum church, crow landing, open passage, courtyard). Bloom can move only in ToneMap 0/2/20/22.
4. **a18 row 0 is shared** by the Kiln (m18_00) and the Asylum (m18_01) in every bank. Any edit to row 0 must satisfy both areas' targets at once, until captures show which rows each bonfire really uses.
5. **Water specular reads the same c5/c6 carrier as the sun.** a16 row 0 (colA_s 200) and row 1 (20) light New Londo's flood water. Cutting them to ≤60 (band max) kills the water sheen ((200/60)^2.2 ≈ ×14 less). Leaving 200 gives capped sun on everything using row 0. That is an open shader issue (section 5). In general, before you zero colA_s on any row, check that row's views for Water-family surfaces.
6. **Lockstep.**
   - The validator always forces same-ID s_LightBank colA* to follow LightBank ratios, colA_s included: 50 → 0 means s_LightBank 100 → 0.
   - The colA_s `max = 60` band also applies to s_LightBank, because the validator strips the `s_` prefix. G5 s_LightBank colA_s is 100 everywhere and the lockstep tolerance is ±10%.
     - A row at vanilla colA_s 50 can be cut to ≤ 33 (s_ at 2×, ≤ 60) or set to 46–55 with s_ left at 100. 34–45 and 56–60 fail.
     - Rows at vanilla 0 can take any value ≤ 60, but only because of an `inf > inf` comparison quirk. Declare it explicitly (README P3).
   - Character rows lockstep only when declared in the manifest. The Archives fix needs a17 char rows 11/18/24 (and 13/25 if used) to be **undeclared**, i.e. independent of their map rows. This is flagged for W1 synthesis.
7. **LightScatteringBank has no band** in global.toml. Until W1 adds one, keep to sunA 0.7–1.4×, sunR/G/B within abs 40 and blendCoef within abs 15.
8. **Row to bonfire mapping is unverified.** The MSBs are in the game folder, which is off limits here. The candidate rows below come from row names and place IDs. Confirm them with a one-row tint or probe capture before tuning. Place IDs: a16 1600/1602 (New Londo), 1601 (boss rows: the Abyss), 1201 (shared transition); a17 1700 (Archives), 1701 (Crystal Cave); a18 1800/1801 (Kiln). Asylum rows carry no place ID.
9. **Sensitivity.** The sweep measured only `archives_balcony` in G5. Other views extrapolate from it and from firelink/parish/anor_interior.

## 4. Areas

### New Londo Ruins (a16): `new_londo`, `new_londo_ingward`

- **Vanilla look.**
  - Strong teal-cyan cast throughout (a −0.03 to −0.04).
  - Misty silhouettes of the drowned city.
  - Map ambient is cyan (150/220/255), fog row 0 is teal (110/160/150) and scatter row 0 is a yellow-white sun at sunA 1300.
  - The Ingward colonnade is darker (p10 = 1, crush 11%).
- **PTDE mood to recover.** There is **no PTDE colour or fog evidence** for New Londo.
  - DSRRL found that DSR needs unusually strong environment, character and fog compensation here to stay visible. It left the whole area out of its PTDE conversion.
  - One ResetEra poster (Ferrs) prefers DSR's New Londo.
  - So the target is **hold vanilla**: no darker, no foggier, no hue shift. Where DSR is "wrong" here, it is wrong at transitions: the Valley of Drakes threshold and the blue void at the gate, caused by a Scattering RGB mismatch with no LightBank blending. Neither is visible from these bonfire cameras.
- **DSR fidelity / modern lighting.** No sun: the ruins sit under a cavern. Optionally lengthen the ShadowBank 0 endDist from 30 to 40–45 for steadier distant shadows (EV-neutral in the sweep).
- **Levers.**
  - LightBank char rows 10/11/13: colA_s 50 → 0, with s_LightBank 10/11/13 colA_s 100 → 0.
  - Map rows 0/1: colA_s stays at vanilla until the water/sun carrier is split (trap 5). Map rows 2–5: colA_s 20 → 0, unless they show water.
  - Do not touch ambient, envDif, fog, ToneMap or ToneCorrect.
  - Candidate rows: Light 0/1 (map), 10/11 (char); Fog 0/1; Scatter 0/1; ToneMap 0/1; ToneCorrect 0/1; Shadow 0.
- **Must not change.**
  - The teal ghost cast and fog colour/density.
  - The flood-water specular before the drain.
  - Visibility of ghosts and the stairs.
  - The Abyss (Four Kings) blackness (rows 3/13; Fog 3 colA 0).

### Duke's Archives (a17): `archives_elevator`, `archives_balcony`

- **Vanilla look.**
  - Clean and pale: blue-white marble floor (23% clipped in the elevator view), gilt balustrades, pink-grey stone, very low saturation (0.16–0.28).
  - Character rows are much brighter than the map rows. Char 11/24 colA_u is 150 against map row 1 at 75, and envSpc is 360.
- **PTDE mood to recover.**
  - DSRRL says DSR over-lights generic indoor characters here, so armour does not sit in the scene. It reduced that lighting.
  - It also found raw PTDE values too dark for the later library and added compensation. So **do not darken the scene** beyond a small amount.
  - It corrected fog colour drift (direction not stated, so no fog change is specified).
  - Mod 711 cut player glow from the outdoor area through to the Crystal Cave.
  - There is **no PTDE colour evidence** for the Archives. Art intent (cold, scholarly, Seath's crystal light) allows at most a slight cool shift, never a warm one.
- **DSR fidelity / modern lighting.**
  - Keep the marble clarity and the specular on armour and gilt. The general notes say DSR armour already reads matte, so char envSpc_colA must not drop below vanilla.
  - Sun off: the elevator hall is indoors, and the balcony bonfire showed an indoor leak in sun1.
  - The bright floor clip must not grow. Reducing it is welcome.
- **Levers.**
  - Character rows (11/24 elevator; 18 or 10 balcony): colA_u ×0.75–0.9, colA_d ×0.85–1.0, envDif_colA ×0.8–1.0. Make them non-lockstep (trap 6). This moves the char crop by about −0.1 to −0.45 EV and the whole frame only slightly.
    - **W1:** only row 11 can take the colA cut (s_ 10).
    - Rows 18 and 24 have s_LightBank colA_u 0, which quantises them to ×0.92–0.98. Cut them with envDif_colA ×0.8–0.9 instead.
    - Keep char envSpc_colA at or above vanilla.
  - Scene: ToneMap grayKey ×0.85–1.0. In the sweep at the balcony, ×1.5 gave +0.46 EV. minLum ×1.0–1.2 (×3 gave −1.04 EV). Combined, grayKey/minAdaptedLum must stay ≥0.78× vanilla, or the readability floor fails.
  - Do not raise ambient or envDif on map rows. At the balcony, ×2 gave +15 and +28 clip points.
  - colA_s → 0 on every a17 row. Rows 0/7/10/17 (exterior and garden) are not covered by any G5 view and stay off until one is captured.
  - Candidate rows:
    - Elevator: Light 1/9 (map), 11/24 (char); Fog 1; Scatter 1; ToneMap 1 (gk 0.08); ToneCorrect 1.
    - Balcony: ToneMap 0 (gk 0.2) with ToneCorrect 0 (sat 0.7) best fits its EV −2.07 and sat 0.16. Otherwise the library rows 8/18/19. Confirm.
- **Must not change.**
  - The pale marble and gold palette.
  - Cool library light against the warm stone arches at the balcony.
  - Readable book and stair detail.
  - Armour and gilt specular.

## 5. Open issues for W1 synthesis and the shader owner

1. **Sun vs water carrier (a16 rows 0/1).** The sun needs a carrier or gate that is independent of Water specular. One option is a build with `DSRLE_SUN_CARRIER_SCATTER`. Another is a rule that rows with water keep their vanilla colA_s and lose the sun some other way. Until one exists, New Londo row 0 users get capped sun. **W1:** resolved as shader gate S1 (row 0 at 200 becomes spec-only). Row 1 (20) is below the cap, so it needs an explicit decision.
2. **Character-row lockstep.** global.toml says that character rows track their map rows within ±10%. The Archives fix needs a17 char rows to be independent. Decide the policy, and declare only the pairs that should track. **W1:** proposed as README P4 (an asymmetric char tolerance of [0.65, 1.10]). The zero s_ fields are fixed separately by P3.
3. **The LightScatteringBank band is missing** in global.toml. Section 2 gives a proposed band. **W1:** README P1.
4. **The a18 row-0 split** between the Kiln and the Asylum, and the **row-to-bonfire confirmation** for all nine views (one tint capture per candidate row set).
5. **The s_LightBank colA_s band and lockstep.** With s_LightBank colA_s at 100, a mid sun (34–45) is unreachable from any vanilla-50 row (trap 6). Fix it one of two ways: exempt colA_s from s_LightBank lockstep (if s_LightBank does not feed the sun carrier), or cap only LightBank colA_s. **W1:** proposed as README P2: the band applies to LightBank only, and lockstep stays. It needs one confirming capture that changes s_LightBank alone.
